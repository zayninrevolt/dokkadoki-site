*The Great Inheritance* presents new work by renowned Mashiko potters Tomoo Hamada and Kan Matsuzaki. The exhibition focuses on two artists connected to major ceramic lineages while developing distinct contemporary practices.

## Visiting

The exhibition runs until **Saturday 17 October** at Keiko Uchida in Notting Hill. General exhibition entry is free. Daily opening hours, step-free access, accessible toilets, seating, parking and photography rules are not published on the exhibition page, so confirm directly before making a special journey.

The launch programme included tea ceremonies, an artist talk and meet-the-artists sessions between 30 September and 3 October. Those timed events have now passed and should not be treated as current booking options.

See the [official exhibition page](https://keikouchida.com/blogs/ceramics/the-great-inheritance) and [online catalogue](https://keikouchida.com/pages/tomoo-hamada-kan-matsuzaki-the-great-inheritance-exhibition-catalogue) for current information.
