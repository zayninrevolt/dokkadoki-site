Learn Tankō Bushi, a Bon dance with roots in the coal-mining communities of Fukuoka, in a beginner-friendly group led through Japan House London. Practice sessions take place on Sunday 23 August and Sunday 20 September, from 14:00 to 15:00.

The group will perform at Japan Matsuri in Trafalgar Square on Sunday 4 October; the public performance time is to be confirmed. The £10 fee includes both practice sessions and the performance.
