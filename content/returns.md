We want you to be happy with anything you buy from Dokkadoki. This page
explains your rights and how our returns process works. Dokkadoki currently
sells **in-store only** — there is no online checkout yet — so this policy
covers purchases made at the café.

## Faulty or misdescribed goods

Under UK consumer law, if an item is faulty, damaged, or not as described,
you have the right to a full refund, repair, or replacement.

Please email us with photos of the fault before returning the item so we can
assist you efficiently.

- You can reject the item within **30 days** of purchase for a full refund.
- After 30 days (and up to 6 months), we will usually offer a repair or
  replacement first. If that is not possible, you will be entitled to a refund.
- Sale or clearance items are covered by the same rights.
- We will cover the cost of return postage for faulty or misdescribed items.

## Change-of-mind returns (in-store purchases)

If you change your mind about a purchase made in-store, you may return it to
us within **30 days**, provided that:

- The item is unused, in the same condition you received it, and preferably
  in its original packaging.
- Some items are not eligible for change-of-mind returns, including:
  - Perishable goods (e.g. food, flowers).
  - Intimate or sanitary products.
  - Hazardous materials, flammable liquids or gases.
  - Gift cards.
  - Downloadable software or digital content once accessed.
  - Certain health and personal care items.
- Proof of purchase is required for all change-of-mind returns.
- Change-of-mind returns must be returned to the café.

## Online purchases

We do not currently sell online. If we introduce online or mail-order sales
in future, the 14-day cooling-off period that applies to distance purchases
under UK consumer law will be added to this page before any online checkout
goes live.

## Memberships and reading passes

Reading passes and memberships are services rather than goods. Once a pass
or membership has been activated at the café, it cannot be cancelled for a
refund, but you are welcome to ask us about transferring or upgrading it —
email [admin@dokkadoki.co.uk](mailto:admin@dokkadoki.co.uk?subject=Return%20or%20exchange) and we'll do
what we can.

## Refund process

Once your return is received and inspected, we will email you to confirm
whether your refund has been approved.

If your return is not approved due to signs of use or damage, we will contact
you to discuss the next steps.

- Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method (or as
  cash for cash purchases) within 14 days.
- Processing times can vary depending on your bank or card provider.
- If you haven't received your refund after this time, please check with your
  bank or card provider, then contact us.

## Exchanges

We only replace items if they are defective or damaged. If you need to
exchange an item, please email us at
[admin@dokkadoki.co.uk](mailto:admin@dokkadoki.co.uk?subject=Return%20or%20exchange) **before** returning it.

- Replacements are subject to stock availability. If a replacement is
  unavailable, a refund will be issued.
- Exchanges for change-of-mind purchases are not available.

## How to return items

Please contact us for our return address.

- For valuable items, we recommend using a trackable shipping service and
  purchasing shipping insurance. Refunds can only be processed once we have
  received the returned products.
- Please include your order number (or proof of purchase) inside the parcel
  so we can process your return promptly.
- We cannot issue a refund for items lost in transit, so we strongly
  recommend using a tracked service.

## Contact us

If you have any questions about refunds and returns, please contact us at
[admin@dokkadoki.co.uk](mailto:admin@dokkadoki.co.uk?subject=Return%20or%20exchange).

Our customer service team is available Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm. We aim to make
your return process as straightforward as possible. Thank you for shopping
with Dokkadoki.
