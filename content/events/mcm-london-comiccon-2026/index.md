MCM London Comic Con returns to ExCeL London for a three-day pop-culture weekend of cosplay, gaming and role-play, creators, Fan Meets, panels and Pop Asia programming.

## What to expect

The organiser highlights Cosplay Central, the Creator Pavilion, Writers Block, gaming and role-play, Fan Meets and guest sessions. Announced guest context includes Critical Role's Matthew Mercer, Marisha Ray and Laura Bailey, plus Nicole Tompkins and Patricia Summersett. Check the [official MCM site](https://www.mcmcomiccon.com/london/en-us.html) for the current guest list and changes.
