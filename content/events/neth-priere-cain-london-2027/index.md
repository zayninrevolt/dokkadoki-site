Japanese visual-kei band **NETH PRIERE CAIN** bring their first European tour to London on Wednesday 17 February 2027. The Underworld date forms part of a nine-city run independently produced by NIPPONGAKU, taking the band from Bratislava through mainland Europe before Madrid and Barcelona.

The London performance is the tour's only announced UK date. A set list, support act and event-specific merchandise have not yet been published.

## The band and its current music

The official profile lists vocalist Itsuki, guitarists Syuri and Daichi, drummer Kenji and bassist Maya. Daichi and Maya joined as full members before the current European tour announcement.

NETH PRIERE CAIN's August 2026 single *相剋の烙印* includes the tracks *PENTAGRAM*, *BAPHOMET* and *VENUS IMAGINATION*. The London set remains TBA, so those releases should not be treated as a confirmed concert programme.

The Underworld is a standing venue with a stated capacity of 500. Its general gig policy is **14+**, with attendees aged 14 or 15 accompanied by an adult.
