Japanese hip-hop artist **Shing02** brings the complete six-part *Luv(sic) Hexalogy* to Manchester Academy 3 on Friday 29 January 2027. Created with the late Japanese producer Nujabes, the series blends reflective lyricism and jazz-rap production. Their separate collaboration *Battlecry* became the opening theme for *Samurai Champloo*, giving Shing02's work a strong connection with anime audiences.

## Performance

The Manchester show pairs Shing02 with special guests. The announced UK touring lineup names **SPIN MASTER A-1** on turntables and **Sam Nakamura** on guitar.

Doors open at **19:30** and the venue lists a **23:00 curfew**. A detailed running order, support set times and event-specific merchandise list have not been published.

## Tickets and entry

General-admission standing tickets are **£26.50 face value** or **£29.15 including the booking fee**. The official selector was accepting orders at the latest check, with a limit of six tickets. A **£1.60 processing fee** and delivery charges from **£1.60 to £6.50 per order** may apply.

## Access and travel

Academy 3 is on the second floor of the University of Manchester Students' Union building. The venue publishes step-free access by ramp and lift, accessible toilets, a hearing loop at the box office and an access area arranged according to demand. Buy a general-admission ticket first, then submit the venue's access requirements form; one companion may enter the access area.
