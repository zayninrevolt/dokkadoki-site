Japan Matsuri returns to Trafalgar Square for a free day celebrating Japanese culture and food. The organiser says 2026 will include returning and new food stalls around the square's fountains, martial arts, cultural activity, and stage performers, with new acts alongside returning favourites.

The 2026 programme is still being announced. The organiser has published its previous-year programme as a guide, but visitors should check the [official Japan Matsuri website](https://japanmatsuri.com/) for this year's final timetable and stall information.

## Visitor information

For safety and capacity management, the organiser prohibits bags larger than A4, bikes and scooters, opened alcohol containers, non-assistance animals, and weapons or bladed items. If Trafalgar Square reaches capacity, entry will operate on a one-in, one-out basis. See the organiser's Conditions of Entry before travelling.

Japan Matsuri is at Trafalgar Square, London WC2N 5DS, from 10am to 8pm.
