The Japan Society's in-person book club discusses *Troubled Waters*, Bryan Karetnyk's English translation of five stories by Ichiyo Higuchi. The collection follows courtesans, children and working-class families living on the margins of Meiji-era Tokyo.

## The book and author

The stories explore poverty, desire, unspoken longing and emotional endurance during a period of rapid social change. Higuchi combines close observation of everyday hardship with psychological insight and social realism.

Higuchi lived from 1872 to 1896 and is widely regarded as Japan's first professional woman writer. Her short career ended with her death from tuberculosis, but her work remains central to modern Japanese literature.
