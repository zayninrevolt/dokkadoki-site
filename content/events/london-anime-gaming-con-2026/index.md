London Anime & Gaming Con fills four floors of Novotel London West with anime, gaming, cosplay, performances and fan activities from 28 to 30 August.

## What to expect

The organiser lists video and tabletop gaming, anime theatre, stage events and panels, cosplay activities, karaoke, parties, exhibitors and merchandise. Published guest and workshop announcements include Valentine Stokes, Synthonie, cyberpunk/VR workshops and Nadia Perrotta of *Cantastorie*. Guests and programming can change, so check the [official event site](https://summer.londonanimecon.com/) before travelling.

The final programme calendar, screening and panel times, and detailed activity schedule are still to be announced.

## Travel, tickets and accessibility

The venue is Novotel London West, International Centre, Shortlands, London W6 8DR. The organiser links to Transport for London and the hotel for travel information; use the [official ticket page](https://summer.londonanimecon.com/tickets) for current prices and availability.

The organiser's accessibility page is still a work in progress. Details including step-free access, carer tickets, quiet space and age/content rules have not been published. Contact `registrations@animeleague.com` before booking if you need support.
