Boris brings the *PINK* 20th-anniversary tour to seven UK cities from 10–16 October. The “Do You Remember The Pink Days?” run revisits the band’s landmark album with Planning for Burial supporting every date, while an added Bristol matinee creates a separate chance to hear *flood*.

## Anniversary releases and an added Bristol show

The tour is part of the current 20th-anniversary cycle around *PINK*. Relapse Records’ official catalogue lists *PINK (20th Anniversary Reissue)* and *Dronevil -Example- (20th Anniversary Reissue)* as current 2×12-inch releases. Exact release dates were not exposed in the catalogue page checked, so they are not described here as newly issued on a specific date.

Bristol now has two separately ticketed performances on 10 October. The added 15:00 matinee is a *flood* set; the later “Pink Days” show is sold out. Boris added the daytime performance after the original Bristol date sold out.
