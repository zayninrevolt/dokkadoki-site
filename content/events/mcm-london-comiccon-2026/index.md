MCM London Comic Con takes over ExCeL London from 23 to 25 October for three days of cosplay, gaming and role-play, creators, Fan Meets, panels and Pop Asia programming.

## What to expect

The organiser lists Cosplay Central, the Creator Pavilion, Writers Block, gaming and role-play, Fan Meets and guest sessions. Announced guests include Critical Role's Matthew Mercer, Marisha Ray and Laura Bailey, plus Nicole Tompkins and Patricia Summersett. Check the [official MCM site](https://www.mcmcomiccon.com/london/en-us.html) for the current guest list and changes.

The final 2026 panel timetable, exhibitor list, trader list and room-by-room programme have not yet been published.

## Opening hours and tickets

- **Friday:** priority entry 10:00-18:00; general entry 12:00-18:00
- **Saturday:** priority entry 09:00-19:00; general entry 11:00-19:00
- **Sunday:** priority entry 10:00-17:00; general entry 12:00-17:00

A Weekend ticket is listed at £98. MCM also lists priority and VIP options; the £300 VIP Legend Pass and £230 VIP Pass were marked sold out on 9 August. Check the [official ticket page](https://www.mcmcomiccon.com/london/en-us/tickets.html) for live prices and availability.

## Plan your visit

MCM publishes dedicated pages for [accessibility](https://www.mcmcomiccon.com/london/en-us/accessibility.html), [cosplay and prop rules](https://www.mcmcomiccon.com/london/en-us/cosplay.html), [getting here](https://www.mcmcomiccon.com/london/en-us/getting-here.html) and family attendance guidance. Carer Pass applicants must complete MCM's approval process before buying a ticket.
