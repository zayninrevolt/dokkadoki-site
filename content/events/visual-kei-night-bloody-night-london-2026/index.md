Visual-kei Night returns to London for an all-night club event dedicated to J-rock, J-metal and Visual-kei. DJs Kaoru Nagata and Hollie lead the music, with gothic, lolita, cosplay and other dramatic fashion welcomed rather than required.

## Tickets and entry

Doors open at **21:00 on Saturday 21 November**. The organiser lists **£15 advance** plus the Eventbrite booking fee, or **£20 on the door**. The event is **18+ and photo ID is required**. A limited free-ticket offer is available to eligible holders of a Sakura Zensen live-show ticket, but the claim form closes at **23:59 on 10 October** and fan-meeting tickets do not qualify.

The organiser asks attendees to read its [house rules](https://www.vkeinight.com/house-rules) and warns that its team may take photos or video for social media. The finish time, re-entry rules, cloakroom, accessibility, quiet-space arrangements and ticket refund terms are not published on the event page. Confirm those details before travelling if needed.
