Dokkadoki is a manga café coming soon to **The Millgate, Bury**: a cosy place to read, relax and discover something new, with Ueshima Coffee, Japanese drinks, self-serve ramen and convenience-store-style snacks.

## Where to find Dokkadoki

Our future home is:

21 Minden Parade<br>
Millgate Shopping Centre<br>
Bury, Lancashire<br>
BL9 0QG
</address>

The café is in Bury town centre, inside The Millgate. [Find the location on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Dokkadoki%2C%2021%20Minden%20Parade%2C%20Bury%20BL9%200QG).

## Before you set off

We are still getting the space ready, so our opening date and regular hours have not been announced yet. Please check the latest update before travelling. Follow us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/dokkadoki/), join the [Dokkadoki Discord](https://discord.gg/zjutK2TTHv), or [join the newsletter](https://dokkadoki.co.uk/#signup) for opening news.

## What to expect

- A welcoming manga café with 1,000+ titles to browse
- Ueshima Coffee, Japanese sodas, matcha and sweet treats
- Self-serve instant ramen cookers with optional toppings
- Sandwiches, onigiri and Japanese convenience-store-style snacks
- A growing manga collection, catalogued on [Libib](https://www.libib.com/u/dokkadoki)
- Manga, anime, Japanese culture and community events
- A curated online [eBay shop](https://www.ebay.co.uk/usr/dokkadokiltd) for anime figures, blind box toys and fan collectibles

## Plan your first visit

Once we open, you will be able to settle in with a manga, choose something from the café menu and spend a little time away from the usual rush. If you already have a favourite series in mind, [request a manga for the shelves](/request/) and help shape what we buy next.

You can also browse our [menu preview](/menupreview/), read about [memberships](/memberships/), and keep an eye on [upcoming events](/events/) while the café takes shape.

## Keep in touch

Opening news, new manga and event updates will appear in the [Dokkadoki blog](/blog/) and newsletter. We send roughly one email a month, and you can leave whenever you like.

For questions, local partnership ideas or accessibility queries, email [admin@dokkadoki.co.uk](mailto:admin@dokkadoki.co.uk?subject=Question).
