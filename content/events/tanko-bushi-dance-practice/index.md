Learn Tankō Bushi, a Bon dance rooted in the coal-mining communities of Fukuoka, in a beginner-friendly group led by Sugiyama Akiko, a Natori-certified Nihon Buyō dancer. The project is part of a collaboration with the Japan Association in the UK's 30th anniversary year.

## Practices and performance

Practice sessions take place in The Hall at Japan House London on Sunday 23 August and Sunday 20 September, 14:00-15:00. The group is expected to perform at Japan Matsuri in Trafalgar Square on Sunday 4 October; the public performance time and meeting arrangements are still to be confirmed closer to the date.

No previous dance experience is needed. Wear comfortable clothing and footwear suitable for light movement; attendance at both practices is strongly encouraged for people planning to perform. Around 25 participants are expected, depending on bookings.

## Tickets, age rules and access

Booking is essential. The £10 fee includes both practices and the group performance; tickets are non-refundable. Under-16s must attend with an adult. Filming or photography may take place.

Japan House has not published event-specific accessibility or travel details for this session. Contact `info@japanhouselondon.uk` or +44 20 3972 7100 before booking if you need support. See the [official Japan House event page](https://www.japanhouselondon.uk/whats-on/learn-to-dance-tanko-bushi-performance-group-for-japan-matsuri/) for booking and updates.
