*Kensho* brings together the work of Japanese ceramicist Taizo Kuroda and calligrapher Bokujin Sousai Inada. The exhibition considers post-war questions of identity, modernity and tradition through Kuroda's white porcelain and Inada's calligraphic and sculptural practice.

## Visiting

The exhibition runs from **1 to 31 October** at Tristan Hoare, 6 Fitzroy Square. Admission is free. Published opening hours are **Tuesday to Friday, 10:30 to 18:00**, and **Saturday, 11:00 to 17:00**. Monday visits are by appointment.
