The Birmingham date of *The Sound of Epic Anime* brings the same orchestral anime programme to Symphony Hall on 15 November. Music from *Naruto*, *One Piece*, *Demon Slayer*, *Attack on Titan*, Studio Ghibli and Makoto Shinkai will fill the hall in a concert focused on the scores rather than a wider convention programme.
