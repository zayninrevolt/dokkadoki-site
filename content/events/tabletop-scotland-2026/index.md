Tabletop Scotland brings board, card, miniature, social, Dungeons & Dragons and roleplaying events to the Royal Highland Centre near Edinburgh.

## What to expect

Visitors can use the games library and Game Finder, explore the Gateway Zone, take part in hobbycraft, browse Bring & Buy, and visit the Playtest Zone and Game Jam. The organiser separates programmed events into board/card/mini/social sessions, D&D and roleplaying games, with exhibitors, food and drink also listed.

## Times, tickets and ages

- **Friday:** 12:00-23:00
- **Saturday:** 10:00-23:00
- **Sunday:** 10:00-17:00 in the main hall; 10:00-19:00 upstairs

Published tickets are £15 Friday, £23 Saturday, £20 Sunday and £40 for the weekend. Do not rely on an older three-day-pass figure: check the [official ticket information](https://tabletopscotland.co.uk/ticket-information/) for current prices, under-16/carer policy and availability.

## Travel and access

The Royal Highland Centre is near Edinburgh Airport; the organiser directs attendees to use the North Gate entry. Parking costs, blue-badge arrangements and detailed accessibility provision should be confirmed with the organiser, as they are not clearly published in the current attendee guidance. See the [official Tabletop Scotland site](https://tabletopscotland.co.uk/) for programme, venue and access updates.
