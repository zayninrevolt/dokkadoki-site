Japanese trio **Mass of the Fermenting Dregs** bring their *Iwaou* world tour to New Century Hall on Saturday 16 October 2027. The Manchester stop sits near the end of a European run that also includes London, Bristol and Glasgow.

## The Manchester show

DICE lists Mass of the Fermenting Dregs as the only announced act, presented by Strange Days. Doors open at **19:00**; a separate stage time, support act and finish time have not been published.

The band's official tour update confirms Manchester as part of the *Iwaou* itinerary. Its latest availability update does not mark Manchester as sold out or low-ticket, but visitors should use the live seller rather than treating that absence as a guarantee.

## Tickets and age limit

DICE displays **£28.46** as “the price you'll pay” and lists the event as **14+**. Tickets are stored in the DICE app. Check the [official ticket page](https://dice.fm/event/g5b2nb-mass-of-the-fermenting-dregs-16th-oct-new-century-manchester-tickets) for current availability before travelling.

DICE says refunds may be available within 24 hours of purchase, or if the event is rescheduled or cancelled. It does not offer refunds within 24 hours of the event start.

## Venue and access

New Century Hall is primarily standing. The venue is cashless, permits one bag no larger than A4 per person, and operates a no re-entry policy after scanning. Manchester Victoria station is approximately five minutes away on foot, with Shudehill tram and bus interchange around two minutes away.

The first-floor Hall has lift access and an accessible viewing platform. New Century provides accessible toilets and a carer or personal-assistant ticket scheme, with advance access registration requested. Read the venue's [visitor information](https://newcenturymcr.com/info) and [accessibility guidance](https://newcenturymcr.com/accessibility) before attending.
