Drink Japan returns to London for a third year on 25 and 26 September, bringing Japanese drinks, food and culture to The Lindley Hall. Visitors can sample hundreds of drinks and food products, meet producers and experts, and buy selected products directly from exhibitors.

The 2026 edition adds a Government of Japan-sponsored Spirits of Japan zone, with shochu tastings, cocktail workshops and awamori from Okinawan distilleries. Eat Tokyo will also serve freshly prepared Japanese dishes during the event.

## Sessions and programme

The organiser marks Friday 25 September, 12:00–16:00 as **trade only and sold out**, and Saturday 26 September, 12:00–16:00 as **sold out**. Public evening sessions are advertised for Friday 25 September, 17:00–21:00 and Saturday 26 September, 17:00–21:00. Eventbrite still displays some session options, so check the live selector for the current position.

The new complimentary masterclasses are registered for at the ticket gate on the day. The provisional timetable lists Friday at 13:00 sake, 14:30 awamori, 18:00 Japanese tea and 19:30 awamori; Saturday at 13:00 awamori, 14:30 sake, 18:00 awamori and 19:30 WSET sake. Session details may change.

The performance programme includes Japanese opera singers Nozomi Fukushima and Takumi Torio, with taiko drummers led by Joji Hirota. The published provisional schedule includes opera and taiko performances across Friday and Saturday, with times listed on the organiser's performance page.

## Tickets and practical information

The organiser’s ticket page gives public prices of £17.50–£35. Eventbrite currently displays Friday evening at £35 and Saturday evening at £38.62 including a displayed £3.62 fee, while some afternoon options remain visible despite the organiser’s sold-out labels. Availability is changing, so check the live selector for the exact session and eligibility. Tickets are for one session, must be booked in advance and cannot be transferred to another session. Eventbrite states that the event is strictly over 18 and offers refunds up to seven days before the event.

Trade entry is complimentary for trade professionals who register online using professional details and present business cards on arrival for a Trade Badge. The organiser says trade is welcome with the public during the Friday evening and both Saturday sessions.

No animals are permitted except guide dogs, and outside food or beverages cannot be brought into the venue. Event-specific parking, accessibility arrangements, carer entry, quiet-space provision, bag rules and arrival guidance are not currently published. Contact the organiser before travelling if you need those details.
