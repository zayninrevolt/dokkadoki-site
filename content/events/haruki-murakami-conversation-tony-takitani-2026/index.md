Haruki Murakami appears at the Barbican on 11 October to read from *The City and Its Uncertain Walls* and discuss his work with interviewer Simon McBurney. The conversation is followed by Jun Ichikawa’s 2004 film *Tony Takitani*, adapted from Murakami’s short story and presented from restored materials.

The film follows a technically gifted illustrator whose isolated life changes when he falls in love, with the story carrying themes of memory and grief. Ryuichi Sakamoto’s spare piano score adds to the film’s distinctive atmosphere. The talk runs for approximately 60 minutes and the film for approximately 75 minutes. The event is in Japanese with English subtitles.
