Stockport Museums' **Create at Staircase** series offers a simple introduction to kintsugi, the Japanese practice of highlighting repairs rather than disguising them. Tutor Caroline Coates leads the two-hour workshop at Staircase House.

## Workshop

This is billed as a **simple version of kintsugi**. Participants will break a small bowl and learn how to repair it, with all materials supplied. The advertised materials and method are not specified as traditional urushi lacquer or gold, so visitors seeking a particular process should check with the organiser before booking.

The workshop is intended to be relaxed and practical. A detailed step-by-step programme and information about taking the repaired bowl home safely have not been published.

## Tickets and safety

Tickets are **£16.96**, and Eventbrite showed **few tickets left** at the latest check. The workshop is for ages **16 and over**. Refund requests are accepted up to seven days before the event.

The organiser warns that the glue may contain nuts and says the workshop is unsuitable for people with nut allergies. Contact Stockport Museums before booking if you need further ingredient or safety information.

## Venue and travel

Staircase House is at 30/31 Market Place in Stockport. Stockport Council describes it as a short walk from Stockport bus and railway stations; the nearest listed car parks are Newbridge Lane and Churchgate, where charges apply.

Event-specific step-free access, companion arrangements and quiet-space information have **not been confirmed** for the workshop. Check the [current ticket page](https://www.eventbrite.co.uk/e/create-at-staircase-simple-kintsugi-tickets-1976905535674) and [Staircase House visitor information](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/planning-your-visit-to-staircase-house) before attending.
