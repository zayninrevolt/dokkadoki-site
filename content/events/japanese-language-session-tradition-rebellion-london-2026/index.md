A beginner-friendly Japanese lesson connects language learning with Japan House London's *Tradition/Rebellion: Coming-of-Age Style in Kitakyushu, Japan* exhibition. Yoshimi Nakane Berrecloth leads the session, with an optional short exhibition tour from Japan House staff afterwards.

## Two repeated sessions

The same lesson runs twice on Saturday 28 November:

- **12:00 to 13:00**
- **14:00 to 15:00**

Each session introduces basic Japanese expressions and is designed for complete beginners or people with very limited Japanese. The optional exhibition tour lasts approximately 15 minutes and is also free.

## Registration and venue

Entry is free, but advance registration is required. Each session is limited to **30 participants**. Registration closes on Thursday **19 November**, or earlier if capacity is reached.

Japan House London is less than 100 metres from High Street Kensington station, served by the District and Circle lines. Its free cloakroom cannot accept luggage larger than a cabin bag, and assistance dogs are welcome throughout the building.

## Before you go

Event-specific age guidance, class materials, quiet-space and carer arrangements are not published. No food, performance, merchandise or signing programme is listed.

The Hall is on the lower-ground floor. Japan House publishes lift access to all floors, accessible toilets and wheelchair availability through its welcome desk.
