Animangapop returns to Cardiff Bay on 28–29 November, open **10:30 to 18:00** each day, with live performances, gaming, cosplay, panels, workshops and a creator market at the Future Inn.

## Programme

The current lineup includes Vocafest, Miu Sleepwalker, IR:INA, DearWish, Fairyl, Kittyrin, Nicole Kirkhouse and Cosmo, with Meow Cosplay judging the cosplay contest. Individual performers appear on selected days, so check the organiser's current schedule before travelling.

The organiser now publishes a dated programme of stage performances, cosplay, panels, gaming and photo opportunities. Times remain subject to change, and visitors are advised to use the Animangapop web app on the day.

## Tickets

The **£12 Early Bird** and **£50 VIP** allocations are sold out. Saturday entry is **£20 and selling fast**, while weekend entry is **£29** and Sunday entry is **£15**. Sales remain live through Eventbrite. Under-16s must be accompanied by a parent or guardian, and the current event page says children under five enter free with a paying adult. Refunds are not generally offered, except for cancellations and specific major changes.

## Access

One free carer pass is available per attendee who needs one, with pre-registration and evidence (DLA/PIP/Blue Badge and similar); assistance animals are welcome. Cosplay prop rules are published on the organiser's site: realistic firearms and metal blades are banned, while foam props and red-tipped lightsabers are allowed.

## Venue and travel

Future Inn Cardiff is approximately a 25-minute walk from Cardiff Central station. The organiser also describes the venue as accessible by car from the M4 and A470; confirm parking arrangements with the hotel before travelling.
