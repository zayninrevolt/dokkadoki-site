Kahchun Wong conducts the Hallé in a programme that opens with Japanese composer Akira Ifukube's *Salome: Dance of the Seven Veils*. Sibelius's Violin Concerto and Walton's choral *Belshazzar's Feast* complete the concert.

## Programme and performers

- Akira Ifukube, *Salome: Dance of the Seven Veils*, approximately 10 minutes
- Sibelius, *Violin Concerto*, approximately 34 minutes
- Walton, *Belshazzar's Feast*, approximately 36 minutes
- Kahchun Wong, conductor
- Alena Baeva, violin
- Roderick Williams, baritone
- Hallé Choir

This is a Japanese-composer crossover listing, not an entirely Japanese programme.
