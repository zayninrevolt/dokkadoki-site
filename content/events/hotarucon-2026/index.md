HotaruCon expands to its first full three-day edition on 13–15 November at the Leonardo Hotel in Milton Keynes, themed "Sport". The 18+ convention packs in cosplay chess, a masquerade, dealers hall and artists alley, a charity cosplay café plus maids-and-butlers café, the FireFly Lounge variety show, a games room, karaoke, nightly parties and a Sunday cosplay ball finale.

**HotaruCon 5 is sold out.** The organiser is accepting names for a first-come-first-served waitlist if spaces become available.

## Tickets

The original £75 ticket covered all three days, including a badge, lanyard, ConBook and ConBag, with panels and events included apart from some pay-to-play charity activities. There are no day tickets or door sales. Joining the waitlist does not guarantee entry; the organiser says any released tickets will be offered on a first-come-first-served basis.

## 2026 schedule

The [full HotaruCon 5 schedule](https://hotarucon.co.uk/page/the-schedule) is now live, with a downloadable [PDF timetable](https://hotarucon.co.uk/files/1791913472_h5-schedule.pdf), Google Calendar and iCal options. Friday includes the opening ceremony, quizzes, panels, gaming and late-night karaoke. Registration opens at 10:00, with 09:00 access for Friday panellists.

Saturday's headline programme includes the cosplay masquerade at 17:15 and FireFly Lounge at 18:45, alongside workshops, quizzes, karaoke and evening DJ sets. The dealers room and artist alley run from 10:00 to 17:00.

Sunday brings cosplay chess at 12:00, the cosplay café from 14:30, the closing ceremony at 17:30 and the Victory Lap cosplay ball at 20:30. Programme times may change, so use the organiser's live schedule when planning the weekend.

## Strictly 18+

Every registered attendee, including carers, must be 18 by the event start; being accompanied by an adult is not enough. Photo ID is required at badge pickup. Cosplay props are checked at the Ops desk, costumes must stay within two metres in any direction, and UK public decency laws apply.

## Access

Free carer tickets are available for paid attendees with evidence (PIP/DLA, Blue Badge, CredAbility Access Card, assistance-dog ID and similar). The con offers priority room access, seating choice, a quiet space, no strobes, ramp access to the stage and free bathroom supplies.
