Visual-kei Night returns to London for an all-night club event dedicated to J-rock, J-metal and Visual-kei. DJs Kaoru Nagata and Hollie lead the music, with gothic, lolita, cosplay and other dramatic fashion welcomed rather than required.

## Music and dress

The night is built around dancing, singing along and hearing Visual-kei tracks at club volume rather than watching a live band. The organiser explicitly welcomes first-time attendees.

Cosplay, gothic, lolita and fancy dress are encouraged, but there is no costume requirement. The event's Bloody Night theme centres dark, theatrical styling.

## Combo night plan

Chaidura's separate show begins at 19:00 at The Black Heart with support from Shirobon and a post-show meet and greet. The combo offer then continues at Visual-kei Night from 21:00 at The Vault.

## Tickets and entry

Doors open at **21:00 on Saturday 21 November**. The organiser lists **£15 advance** plus the Eventbrite booking fee, or **£20 on the door**. The event is **18+ and photo ID is required**. The earlier free-ticket offer has ended. A new **20% combo-ticket deal** covers Chaidura's 19:00 show at The Black Heart and Visual-kei Night at The Vault from 21:00. New buyers can select the combo through either Eventbrite page; anyone who already holds one ticket can request a code for the other event from Chaidura or Visual-kei Night.
