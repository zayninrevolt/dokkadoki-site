Japanese speakers of different nationalities meet in Carnaby for an informal evening of conversation on Tuesday 1 December.

The gathering is part of the group's regular first-Tuesday series.

## Language level and format

Intermediate Japanese is recommended. This is a social language gathering rather than a taught beginner lesson.

The meeting starts at **19:00** and usually continues until around **23:00**. A buffet is available, with a small payment collected at the door to cover its cost.

## No advance booking

There is no booking form or advance ticket: attendees can turn up and pay on arrival. The exact door payment is not published, so bring a suitable payment method and confirm current details through the organiser before travelling.

## Before you go

This is not a structured class, examination-preparation session or one-to-one tuition. No formal programme, named speaker, workshop, merchandise or performance is advertised.

The group meets at The Red Lion, 14 Kingly Street, Carnaby, London W1B 5PR. Programme details may change.

Parking, accessibility, age guidance and refund terms are not published on the event page. See the [Japan Society listing](https://www.japansociety.org.uk/event?event=1031&eventcat=11) and [Japanese Conversation Group website](http://jcg.org.uk/) for updates.

The organiser does not publish a last-entry time, dress code or quiet-space arrangement.
