Jodo Shinshu UK's **Hōonkō 2027** is a public weekend memorial for Shinran Shōnin, the founding figure of the Jodo Shinshu Buddhist tradition. The Manchester gathering combines services and teaching with discussion, practical workshops and informal time together.

## Four-part weekend programme

The event is organised across four blocks: Friday evening, Saturday morning, Saturday afternoon to evening, and Sunday morning. Each includes a service, Dharma talk and discussion led by one of four Jodo Shinshu UK priests: Rev. David Quirke-Thornton, Rev. Louella Matsunaga, Rev. Caroline Brazier and Rev. Enrique Galván-Álvarez.

Workshops will cover altar-building and other practical matters. A full block-by-block timetable and individual workshop times are **not yet published**. The overall weekend runs from **18:00 on Friday 22 January to 12:00 on Sunday 24 January**.

## Tickets, food and accommodation

Entry is **free**, and the current Eventbrite offer is listed as available. Jodo Shinshu UK welcomes optional donations towards venue and refreshment costs. The listing states that bookings are non-refundable.

Tea, coffee and light refreshments will be provided, but there is no full food service. The weekend is non-residential, so visitors must arrange their own accommodation. The organiser notes nearby options and food businesses around Manchester's Curry Mile, but neither is included with admission.

## Venue and practical information

Birch Community Centre is on Brighton Grove in Manchester and the listing includes free parking. Event-specific age guidance, step-free access, accessible toilets, companion arrangements and quiet-space information are **not published**; contact Jodo Shinshu UK before booking if these affect attendance.

See the [current organiser listing](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/jodo-shinshu-uks-hoonko-2027-from-manchester-tickets-2001406037334) for booking status and the promised detailed schedule closer to the weekend.
