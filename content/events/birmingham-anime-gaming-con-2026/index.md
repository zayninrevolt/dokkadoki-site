Birmingham Anime & Gaming Con returns for a two-day edition at Edgbaston Stadium on 19–20 September. The current programme spans anime, video and tabletop gaming, cosplay, karaoke, parties, panels, performers and exhibitors, with a ten-person guest and performer line-up already advertised.

## What is announced for 2026

The organiser currently names Debi Derryberry, Aileen Mythen, AfroGeek House, Murdah Srvc, Amber, LELE, MiuoniVT, SYNTHONIE, DJ Manimal and SOSOSO_tokyo_berlin. Newly advertised material includes AfroGeek House’s anime game show on the main stage and SYNTHONIE’s live cyberpunk music with interactive VR music workshops.

Confirmed activity areas include video gaming, anime theatre, tabletop gaming, stage events and panels, cosplay, karaoke and parties. A dated Birmingham timetable and exact closing/party times are still **not published / confirm with organiser**.

## Tickets and entry

The accessible live selector showed weekend tickets at £30, Saturday at £22 and Sunday at £15, all marked in stock when checked. Standard entry begins at 11:30. Early Bird adds £6, with entry from 10:00 and five raffle tickets. VIP adds £15 and includes Early Bird benefits, ten raffle tickets, a convention badge, official lanyard and tote bag. Mandatory checkout fees were not exposed, so totals should be confirmed before purchase.

The organiser’s general policy says the event is all ages, with most content approximately PG-13. Under-16s need a responsible adult aged 18 or over; children aged eight and under enter free with an adult. Some evening content may be 18+ and require photo ID.

All weapons and large props are checked. Real weapons, metal replicas, sharp-tipped items, realistic imitation firearms, projectile-firing props and pyrotechnics are prohibited. The organiser may refuse oversized or heavy props; event and venue staff have the final decision.

## Access and travel

The organiser describes its accessibility process as a work in progress and directs access queries to registrations@animeleague.com. Specific Edgbaston routes, companion arrangements, cloakroom provision, parking and event-day transport guidance are **not published / confirm with organiser**. See the [official event website](https://summer.birminghamanimecon.com/) before travelling.
