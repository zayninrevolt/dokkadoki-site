## Exhibition focus

*The Great Inheritance* presents new work by renowned Mashiko potters Tomoo Hamada and Kan Matsuzaki. The exhibition focuses on two artists connected to major ceramic lineages while developing distinct contemporary practices.

The launch talk explored how relationships to their fathers and grandfather, themselves well-known potters, have influenced the artists' work and careers.
