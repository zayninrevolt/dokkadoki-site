Tabletop Scotland brings board, card, miniature, social, Dungeons & Dragons, and roleplaying events to the Royal Highland Centre. Visitors can use the games library and Game Finder, explore the Gateway Zone, take part in hobbycraft, browse Bring & Buy, and visit the Playtest Zone and Game Jam.

The official site separates programmed events into board/card/mini/social sessions, D&D events, and roleplaying-game events. It also lists exhibitors, food and drink, attendee terms, and a code of conduct.
