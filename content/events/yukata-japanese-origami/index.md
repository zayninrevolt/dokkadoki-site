Discover two Japanese cultural traditions in one hands-on workshop. Learn the basics of wearing a yukata and fold origami, from classic cranes to more intricate paper forms, while exploring customs for respectful travel in Japan.

This session is aimed at newcomers and provides a relaxed introduction to Japanese dress, paper craft, and cultural etiquette.
