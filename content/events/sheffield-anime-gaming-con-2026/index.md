Sheffield Anime & Gaming Con returns to Mercure Sheffield on 10 and 11 October with a programme that puts live performance and hands-on play alongside the convention staples. The current guest list includes cyberpunk music artist SYNTHONIE, DJ Manimal, VTuber and animator CynnVAL, band Going Live & Kicking, cosplay dance performer Shiro.chuu and dancer Tiger.

## What to expect

SYNTHONIE brings cyberpunk live music and interactive VR music workshops, while the wider programme includes video gaming, retro games, tournaments, more than 100 board games, RPGs and TCG tournaments. Anime theatre, stage events, panels, cosplay activities, karaoke and parties are also advertised, alongside the Cosplay Masquerade, Lip Sync Battle, Talent Show, Anime Bingo and Big Geek Quiz.

## Tickets and entry

The official ticket selector lists a weekend ticket at £30, Saturday at £22 and Sunday at £15. Early Bird adds £6 and includes entry from 10:00 plus five raffle tickets; VIP adds £15 and includes the Early Bird benefits, ten raffle tickets, a con badge, lanyard and tote bag. Standard entry is from 11:30. Children under 8 enter free, and under-16s must be accompanied by a paying adult. Eventbrite lists the event headline as 11:00–22:00 and refunds up to seven days before the event. Check the official selector for the final basket total and current availability.

Mercure Sheffield is at 119 Norfolk Street, Sheffield S1 2JE.
