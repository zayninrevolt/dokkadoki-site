A visiting Ise City programme brings the culture around Ise Jingu's periodic shrine rebuilding to Cambridge. After an introduction to the city and shrine, participants make a *Zai*, the ceremonial baton used during the *Okihiki* procession that carries sacred timber for reconstruction.

## Rebuilding, renewal and craft

Ise Jingu's *Shikinen Sengu* rebuilds shrine structures on adjacent plots every 20 years before the sacred deity is transferred to the new home. The process preserves craft skills while expressing a Shinto tradition of spiritual renewal. The next rebuild is planned for 2033.

KAWANISHI Masahiko of Miyachu Ltd leads the hands-on *Zai* workshop after the cultural introduction.
