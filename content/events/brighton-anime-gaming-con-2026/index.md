Brighton Anime & Gaming Con brings a broad anime, gaming and live-performance programme to Brighton Racecourse on 21 and 22 November. The current lineup includes *Danganronpa* voice actor Brian Beacock, Penelope Rawlins, cyber-pop idol LELE, cyberpunk performer SYNTHONIE and the interactive video game concert Extra Lives.

The organiser also lists Murdah Srvc, described as Massive Attack meets *Ghost in the Shell*, alongside video gaming, anime theatre, tabletop gaming, stage events, panels, cosplay, karaoke, parties, exhibitors and merchandise. No named food vendors are published.

## Tickets and entry

The official ticket selector currently displays a full-weekend price of £30 alongside a £37 reference, Saturday at £22 and Sunday at £15. Early Bird adds £6 and VIP adds £15. Standard entry starts at 11:30, while Early Bird entry starts at 10:00. Children under 8 enter free and under-16s must be accompanied by a paying adult.
