Comic Con Liverpool returns to the Liverpool Experience Campus on Saturday 10 and Sunday 11 October, with venue-listed opening times of 09:00–18:00.

## What to expect

The organiser and venue advertise celebrity photo opportunities, autographs and live Q&A panels, cosplay competitions, retro and current gaming zones, immersive props and set builds, photo opportunities, artists and shopping on a large show floor.

Named guests, exhibitors, food vendors, a detailed timetable, tabletop programming and tournament formats are **not published / confirm with organiser**.

## Tickets and planning

Under-14s must attend with a paying adult. Attendees aged 14 and over may attend unaccompanied at parent or guardian discretion. Ticket prices, tier benefits, fees, refund policy, bag/security rules, props policy and sold-out status are **not published / confirm with organiser**.

The [official venue listing](https://www.exhibitioncentreliverpool.com/whats-on/comic-con-liverpool/) links to travel, parking and accessibility information, but event-specific arrangements were not stated in the listing. Check the [official organiser website](https://www.comicconventionliverpool.co.uk/) before travelling.
