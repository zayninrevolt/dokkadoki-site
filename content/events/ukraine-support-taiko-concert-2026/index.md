The Japan-Ukraine Sakura Peace Initiative presents a community fundraising concert featuring London Taiko Drummers, founded by taiko master Joji Hirota, and St Mary's Ukrainian School Choir. Funds are intended to support Japanese cherry trees and other aid for Ukraine in spring 2027.

## Tickets and timing

Doors open at **13:00** and the concert begins at **13:30 on Sunday 1 November**. The finish time is not published. Tickets are **£15 for adults**, **£10 for ages 5 to 16**, and free for children under five. The organiser says children aged five and under should sit on an adult's lap. Booking is through the [official application form](https://forms.gle/5ph2sM6miWZ7aXPX6).

## Important address correction

The booking form currently says "Church Road, SW15 6SN", but St Mary's Church and the Japan Society list the venue as **Putney High Street, London SW15 1SN**, next to Putney Bridge. The church's own address is the stronger venue source. Because the organiser's form conflicts, confirm the location directly before travelling rather than using SW15 6SN.

Accessibility, companion tickets, seating, refund terms, parking and payment protection are not published. The form requests payment by bank transfer, so review its terms carefully before sending money.
