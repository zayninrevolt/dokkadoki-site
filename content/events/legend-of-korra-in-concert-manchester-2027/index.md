*The Legend of Korra in Concert* brings the animated series to Manchester with HD projection and its score performed live by an orchestra. Composer Jeremy Zuckerman's music blends classical orchestration with jazz, electronic and world-music influences.

## Live-to-picture programme

The concert is tied to the series' 15th anniversary. The venue has not published a detailed scene list, named orchestra, conductor, interval or running time.

This is a seated concert rather than a convention. No cosplay competition, panel, workshop, gaming area, guest signing or merchandise programme is advertised.

## Tickets and entry

The performance begins at **19:30** on Monday 1 February. Doors, interval and finish times remain **TBC**.

Official prices run from **£38.25 to £74.25**, including the venue's stated **£4.75 booking fee**. A Premium Lounge upgrade is advertised. The ticket selector could not be independently checked, so confirm current seat availability with the venue.

The organiser has not published event-specific age guidance, carer arrangements or quiet-space information.

Venue rules include:

- Bags should be no larger than approximately A3.
- Outside food and drink are not permitted except one sealed plastic water bottle up to 500ml.
- Late admission may be delayed.

## Access and travel

Wheelchair and other access bookings are handled through the venue. The city-centre hall is close to St Peter's Square Metrolink stop, with nearby rail, bus and paid parking options described in its visitor guidance.
