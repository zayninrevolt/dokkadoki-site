Brighton Anime & Gaming Con brings a broad anime, gaming and live-performance programme to Brighton Racecourse on 21 and 22 November. The current lineup includes *Danganronpa* voice actor Brian Beacock, Penelope Rawlins, original *Sonic the Hedgehog* artist Duncan Gutteridge, cyber-pop idol LELE, cyberpunk performer SYNTHONIE and the interactive video game concert Extra Lives.

DJ Manimal, panelist Dayna Del Mar and actor and voice artist Xander Phillips are also listed. The programme includes video gaming, anime theatre, tabletop gaming, stage events, panels, cosplay, karaoke, parties, exhibitors and merchandise. Murdah Srvc is not listed on the current guest page, so confirm directly with the organiser before planning around that appearance. No named food vendors are published.

## Guest highlights

Brian Beacock is known for Monokuma in *Danganronpa*, Akutagawa in *Bungo Stray Dogs* and Takato in *Digimon*. Penelope Rawlins' game work includes *Genshin Impact*, *Baldur's Gate 3*, *Horizon Forbidden West* and *Arknights*.

Duncan Gutteridge is one of the original *Sonic the Hedgehog* artists. Extra Lives turns video-game music into an interactive concert, while SYNTHONIE combines cyberpunk live performance with interactive VR music workshops.

LELE brings Harajuku-inspired cyber-pop, and DJ Manimal joins the music programme. The final timetable and individual appearance times remain to be published.

## Tickets and entry

The official ticket selector currently displays a full weekend from **£30**, Saturday from **£22** and Sunday from **£15**. Early Bird adds £6 and VIP adds £15. Standard entry starts at 11:30, while Early Bird entry starts at 10:00. Children aged 8 and under enter free and under-16s must be accompanied by a responsible adult aged 18 or over.
