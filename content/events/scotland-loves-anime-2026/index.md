Scotland Loves Anime returns across three cities: Glasgow Film Theatre from **6 to 8 November**, Picturehouse Central London from **13 to 15 November**, and Edinburgh Filmhouse from **16 to 22 November**. Tickets are now on sale.

## Programme

[Glasgow's published programme](https://www.lovesanimation.com/cinema/glasgow) opens with the UK premieres of *The Obsessed* and *To You in the Beyond*, followed by the Scottish premiere of the three-film anthology *Grotesqqque* and a 4K restoration of *Ninja Scroll*.

The weekend continues with *A Silent Voice*, *Ghost*, *A New Dawn*, *Samurai Ballerina*, *Cocoon* with a bonus documentary, and *We Are Aliens*. The organiser also highlights appearances by *Ghost* director Shingo Natsume and *Shiranui* director Ryo Katanosaka.

## Glasgow screening times

Friday's programme starts with *The Obsessed* at 12:40, followed by *To You in the Beyond* at 15:10, *Grotesqqque* at 18:00 and *Ninja Scroll* at 20:40.

Saturday brings *A Silent Voice* at 15:00, *Ghost* at 18:00 and *A New Dawn* at 20:40. Sunday's screenings are *Samurai Ballerina* at 14:00, *Cocoon* at 17:00 and *We Are Aliens* at 19:00.

## Tickets and practicalities

All 2026 tickets are on sale through the screening links on the [official festival website](https://www.lovesanimation.com/). Glasgow also offers a weekend pass alongside individual film tickets. Prices and age ratings vary by screening, so check the selected cinema page before booking.

The festival is a registered charity (SC041567) run by Scotland Loves Animation.
