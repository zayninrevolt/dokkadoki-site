Dokkadoki is a manga café and reading room **coming soon to The Millgate, Bury**: a cosy little Japanese escape where you can settle in with a good read, enjoy a drink, and discover something new.

## Before you set off

We are still getting the space ready, so our opening date and regular hours have not been announced yet. Follow us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/dokkadoki/) or join the conversation on [Discord](https://discord.gg/zjutK2TTHv) for the latest news.

## What to expect

- A welcoming place to read manga, relax and spend time with fellow fans
- A growing manga collection; browse the current catalogue on [Libib](https://www.libib.com/u/dokkadoki)
- Manga and anime-inspired events as plans take shape
- A café menu, with more details to come
- A small online [eBay shop](https://www.ebay.co.uk/usr/dokkadoki) for manga and related finds

## Help shape Dokkadoki

Got a manga series you would love to see on the wall? [Send us a manga request](/request/). We use requests to help guide what we look for next.

For questions or local partnership ideas, email us at [admin@dokkadoki.co.uk](mailto:admin@dokkadoki.co.uk).
