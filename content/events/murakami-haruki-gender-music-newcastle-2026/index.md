Three Japanese Studies researchers examine gender, narrator types and music across Haruki Murakami’s writing.

## A new novel as the starting point

The talk is prompted by the Japanese publication of his 2026 novel *The Tale of Kaho* and avoids plot details from the new work.

## Speakers and themes

Dr Gitte Marianne Hansen opens with gender criticism and narrator types leading up to Murakami’s latest work. PhD candidate Yueying Wu compares representations of the female body in *The Wind-Up Bird Chronicle* and the short story “Kaho”. Dr Matteo Giacchè closes with the ways music shapes narrators, characters and narrative construction across Murakami’s fiction.

The speakers bring research spanning gender and character construction, transnational female embodiment, manga, music and Japanese literary studies.
