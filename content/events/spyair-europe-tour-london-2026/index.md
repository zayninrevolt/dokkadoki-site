SPYAIR bring their first European tour of 2026 to a London finale at Scala. The Nagoya rock band’s catalogue includes anime songs from *Haikyuu!!*, *Gintama* and *Bleach*, including “Imagination”, “I’m a Believer”, “Orange” and “Samurai Heart (Some Like It Hot!!)”.

## Show and VIP timings

VIP doors open at **18:00**, general doors at **19:00**, and SPYAIR are due on stage at **20:00**. Scala lists the event ending at **22:00**. No support act has been announced.

The VIP ticket includes early entry, early merchandise access, a meet and greet, an autograph and a photo. VIP tickets are sold separately through Ticket Tailor, while general admission is sold through DICE.

## Tickets and entry

DICE lists general admission at **£53.94** and describes that as the final price. Tickets are currently on sale. DICE says refunds are available within 24 hours of purchase, or if the event is cancelled or rescheduled, but not within 24 hours of the event start.

This is a **16+ event with no exceptions**. Scala may require original physical photo ID and uses ID scanning for this show. The venue is standing and tickets do not include reserved seating.

## Venue access and practical information

Scala has many stairs and no lift. Visitors who need advance access, companion admission or limited seating should contact the venue after buying a ticket. Wheelchair access may require manual assistance, and the venue says heavy electric wheelchairs may not be possible to carry safely.

There is no re-admission. Outside food and drink are not allowed, although Scala has bars and free tap water. Check [Raku Events](https://raku.events/concerts/spyair-2026), the [Scala event page](https://scala.co.uk/events/spyair/) and [Scala admission guidance](https://scala.co.uk/admission/) before travelling.
