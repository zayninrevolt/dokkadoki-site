Boris celebrates *PINK*’s 20th anniversary across seven UK cities from 10–16 October. The “Do You Remember The Pink Days?” run revisits the album with Planning for Burial supporting every date, while an added Bristol matinee creates a separate chance to hear *flood*.

## Anniversary releases and an added Bristol show

The tour is part of the current 20th-anniversary cycle around *PINK*. Relapse Records’ official catalogue lists *PINK (20th Anniversary Reissue)* and *Dronevil -Example- (20th Anniversary Reissue)* as current 2×12-inch releases, although it does not give exact release dates.

Bristol now has two separately ticketed performances on 10 October. The added 15:00 matinee is a *flood* set; the later “Pink Days” show is sold out. Boris added the daytime performance after the original Bristol date sold out.

## UK dates and availability

- **10 October - Strange Brew, Bristol:** added 15:00 *flood* set; official artist schedule did not mark it sold out
- **10 October - Strange Brew, Bristol:** late “Pink Days” set - **sold out**
- **11 October - Digital, Newcastle:** no sold-out annotation on the artist schedule; confirm live availability
- **12 October - Òran Mór, Glasgow:** **sold out**
- **13 October - Brudenell Social Club, Leeds:** **sold out**
- **14 October - Gorilla, Manchester:** **sold out**
- **15 October - Electric Brixton, London:** **low-ticket alert**
- **16 October - Concorde 2, Brighton:** **sold out**

The artist’s status annotations do not establish live seller inventory or current prices and mandatory fees, so confirm each date with the relevant seller. Full running orders, support stage times, set lengths and merchandise details remain TBA.
