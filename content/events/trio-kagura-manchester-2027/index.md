Manchester-formed Trio Kagura brings together musicians from Japan, Lithuania and New Zealand for a lunchtime concert at The Bridgewater Hall. Its name comes from *kagura*, the Japanese tradition of ritual music and dance.

## Performers and programme

- Pijus Jonušas, violin
- Kanako Mizuno, piano
- Yuuki Bouterey-Ishido, cello
- Britten, *Introduction and Allegro*
- Ravel, *Piano Trio in A minor*

The venue also notes the ensemble's recent Britten-Pears Young Artists residency, New Zealand tour and teaching invitation in Delft.

## Times and tickets

Auditorium doors open at **12:45**. The concert runs from **13:00 to 13:50** on Monday 10 May.
