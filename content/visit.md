Dokkadoki is a manga café **coming soon to The Millgate, Bury**: a cosy corner featuring Ueshima Coffee and self-serve Japanese convenience-store-style food including instant ramen cookers with optional toppings, plus sandwiches, onigiri and Japanese treats - where you can settle in with a good read and discover something new.

## Before you set off

We are still getting the space ready, so our opening date and regular hours have not been announced yet. Follow us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/dokkadoki/) or join the conversation on [Discord](https://discord.gg/zjutK2TTHv) for the latest news.

## What to expect

- A welcoming manga café with 1,000+ titles to browse
- Ueshima Coffee and Japanese treats
- Self-serve instant ramen cookers with optional toppings, plus sandwiches and onigiri
- A growing manga collection; browse the current catalogue on [Libib](https://www.libib.com/u/dokkadoki)
- Manga and anime-inspired events as plans take shape
- A small online [eBay shop](https://www.ebay.co.uk/usr/dokkadokiltd) for manga and related finds
