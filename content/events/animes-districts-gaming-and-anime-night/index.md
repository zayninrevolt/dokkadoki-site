Anime District's Gaming and Anime Night is an over-18s anime-and-gaming social at Platform Shoreditch. It runs 16:00-22:00; last entry is 20:00 and entry is not guaranteed after that time.

## Tickets and entry

Valid ID and an e-ticket/QR code are required. By 9 August the £18.59 Early Bird tier had ended; General Admission is £22.38 including the £2.38 fee, with sales scheduled to end on 5 September subject to availability. Eventbrite currently labels the event **Going fast**. Refunds are available until seven days before the event. Check the [official Eventbrite listing](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/anime-districts-gaming-and-anime-night-tickets-1984722893602) for live availability.

## What to expect

The organiser advertises *The Sims 4* base game on PCs and PS5s, plus other PC, PS5 and Nintendo Switch stations. Guests may bring handheld consoles at their own risk to play with others. Tabletop options include board games, a special-edition Sims Game of Life and custom anime games.

The programme also includes anime opening themes and soundtracks, Simlish karaoke, photo opportunities and a raffle giveaway. A stocked bar, specialised cocktails and food are available to buy.

## Cosplay, travel and access

Cosplay is invited. Guests can dress as a favourite anime character or wear a plumbob; people without one are promised a free plumbob headband on entry.

The Eventbrite page provides map directions for walking, cycling, public transport and driving. Contact the organiser before attending if you need access or parking arrangements, prop guidance, or exact game, karaoke and raffle details.
