Japanese reggae-punk outfit SiM headline seven UK dates in January 2027, touring their seventh album *HOOMAN AFTER ALL*. The run includes intimate club shows in Cardiff and Bristol, with the band best known internationally for *The Rumbling*, the *Attack on Titan* opener that became a US number-one rock single.

## Tickets

General tickets are on sale now, roughly £25–£26 face value plus per-venue fees. Venues generally restrict entry to 14+ with under-16s accompanied by an adult (a couple of venues allow under-16s with an adult without a strict age floor; London requires valid physical photo ID and allows one small bag up to A4). Confirm the exact terms for your date with the ticket seller.

A separate VIP meet-and-greet is £89 via Soundrink and does **not** include concert admission. It covers a meet-and-greet, individual photo op, signed exclusive vinyl variant, souvenir laminate with lanyard, enamel pin set, tote bag, early entry and pre-show merch access. VIP details are emailed two to three days before the show and all items are collected at the venue.

## About the tour

*HOOMAN AFTER ALL* is the band's seventh full-length, released 2 September 2026 on UNFD. The world tour opened in Japan in September 2026, crossed North America in autumn, and reaches the UK and Europe in early 2027 before finishing in Barcelona on 16 February. Recent singles include *BLiNDEYES* with Yukina of HANABIE. and the *Black Torch* opening theme *FREEZE ME UP*.
