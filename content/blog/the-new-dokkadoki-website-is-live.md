The new Dokkadoki website is live.

It is the best place to keep up with what we are building in Bury, find an upcoming event, and see the ways you can get involved before the café opens.

## Start here

- [Plan your visit](/visit/) for what we can share so far about the café, the kind of food and drink we are planning, and where to find us at The Millgate.
- [Browse upcoming events](/events/) for anime, manga and Japanese culture events around the UK. Each listing has the practical details we can verify, plus a link to the organiser for tickets and updates.
- [Read about memberships](/memberships/) if you would like to follow along as Dokkadoki takes shape.
- [Request a manga](/request/) if there is something you would love to see on the shelves. Requests help us decide what to look for next.

## Join the conversation

Our [Discord community](https://discord.gg/zjutK2TTHv) is a good place to chat, share recommendations and hear about new things as they happen. It is still growing, so come say hello.

## Our eBay shop

We also have a [Dokkadoki eBay shop](https://www.ebay.co.uk/usr/dokkadokiltd) with anime figures, blind box toys and fan collectibles. Stock changes, so it is worth checking back if you are looking for something fun for your shelf.

There is more on the way. For now, have a look around, join the Discord if you fancy it, and keep an eye on the Events page.
