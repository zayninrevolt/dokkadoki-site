Japanese novelist Nao-cola Yamazaki tours the UK in October with the Japan Foundation, appearing at two literature festivals plus bookshop dates across England. Her novel *Don't Laugh at Other People's Sex Lives* was a Japanese bestseller, and her latest work *Beautiful Distance* is out from Daunt Books.

## Confirmed dates

- **Sunday 18 October, 17:00** - Cheltenham Literature Festival, Pillar Room, Cheltenham Town Hall, £16, 60 minutes, chaired by Rosie Goldsmith with a book signing. Live captions, step-free entrance, guide dogs welcome and accessible toilets.
- **Monday 19 October, 18:30** - Manchester Literature Festival, Waterstones Deansgate, £10/£8, co-presented with the Japan Foundation and Waterstones. Wheelchair access with free essential carer tickets via the box office (0161 876 2015).

- **Wednesday 21 October, 19:00** - Daunt Books, Marylebone High Street, London.
- **Thursday 22 October, 17:00** - Pembroke College, University of Oxford.
- **Friday 23 October, 18:30** - Storysmith Books, Bristol.
- **Saturday 24 October, 15:00** - Off the Shelf Festival of Words, Sheffield.

Registration is now open for all six appearances. Use the individual **Book Now** links on the [Japan Foundation tour page](https://lo.jpf.go.jp/whats-on/meet-the-author-nao-cola-yamazaki/) to check each venue's current price, availability and access information.

## Before travelling

Prices are published above only where the official festival listings state them. Running times, signing arrangements and venue access differ by stop, so use the selected booking page rather than assuming one tour-wide policy.
