Tokonatsu is an outdoor Japanese pop-culture camping festival: a long weekend for anime, manga, cosplay, games and community events at Boyd Campsite and Activity Centre.

## What to expect

The organiser's current programme areas include cosplay, crafting, panels, indoor and outdoor events, arcade and tabletop gaming, TokoMart, Bring & Buy, dealers and artisans, a food court, matsuri and campfire activities. The Food Court serves breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks through the event. The final dated 2026 timetable, named food traders and individual session details are still to be announced, so check the [official schedule](https://www.tokonatsu.org.uk/whats-on/schedule/) before travelling.

## Tickets, camping and arrival

Published ticket waves are: Early Access Thursday-Monday £85/£95/£105 (£115 on the day); Standard Entry Friday-Monday £65/£75/£85 (£95 on the day); Saturday £30/£40/£50 (£60 on the day); Sunday £25/£35/£45 (£55 on the day); and two-day entry £50/£60/£70 (£80 on the day). Ages 10-15 can use U16 tickets and under-10s enter free; both must attend with an eligible adult ticket holder.

"Pay later" orders must be paid within 14 days. The organiser lists card payment, vouchers and Klarna options. Free one-hour drop-off slots are being trialled: Thursday 15:00-20:00 and Friday 11:00-20:00. See the [official ticket information](https://www.tokonatsu.org.uk/tickets/info/) for current availability and terms.

## Travel, parking and access

On-site parking for Tokonatsu 2026 is sold out. Arlesey & Henlow station is about a 15-20 minute walk; do not park in nearby residential streets. Blue Badge arrangements are described inconsistently on the organiser's pages, so contact Tokonatsu directly before relying on a disabled bay.

This is an outdoor campsite. Permanent toilets/showers and an additional unisex portable shower are planned, but visitors with health or mobility requirements should contact the organiser early to discuss what support is possible. More [venue, arrival and parking information](https://www.tokonatsu.org.uk/venue/) is available from Tokonatsu.
