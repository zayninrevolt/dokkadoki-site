Kenji Fukasaku’s landmark 2000 film *Battle Royale* returns to the Barbican on 10 October with an introduction by lead actor Tatsuya Fujiwara. Fujiwara played Shuya Nanahara, the student who refuses to accept the deadly government programme at the centre of the film.

**Sold out:** the Barbican currently marks this screening as sold out. The film is classified 18.

Set in a dystopian near-future Japan, the film follows 42 senior school pupils forced onto a remote island and ordered to kill or be killed. The Barbican describes it as blood-drenched, savage and darkly funny, and lists a runtime of 1 hour 54 minutes. The film is directed by Kenji Fukasaku and originated in Japan.
