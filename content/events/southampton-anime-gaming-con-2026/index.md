AnimeLeague's Southampton con returns to the Novotel on 31 October–1 November with two standout headline acts: **Extra Lives**, an interactive video-game concert, and **SYNTHONIE**, whose cyberpunk live set pairs with interactive VR music workshops.

## What's on

Beyond the headliners the weekend covers video gaming, anime theatre, tabletop gaming, stage panels, cosplay activities, karaoke and parties. The programme includes a cosmasquerade, lip sync battle, talent show, anime bingo, a big geek quiz, voice-actor guests, boardgame and TCG tournaments, live bands and after-parties.

The [published timetable](https://autumn.southamptonanimecon.com/timetable) now gives times across the main stage, video gaming, tabletop gaming and anime-screening rooms. Saturday includes SYNTHONIE at 13:00, *Extra Lives: Interactive Video Game Concert* at 15:00 and the cosplay masquerade at 17:00. Sunday includes a Sonic the Hedgehog artist Q&A with Duncan Gutteridge at 13:00 and the cosplay masquerade at 17:00. The organiser recommends its app for live changes or cancellations.

## Timetable highlights

Saturday's screening room lists *Arrietty*, *Dragon Ball Z*, *Pokémon: Secrets of the Jungle* and *Naruto*. Sunday lists *My Neighbour Totoro*, *My Hero Academia* and *Psycho-Pass*.

The gaming rooms schedule *Smash Bros. Ultimate*, *Mario Kart*, Minecraft, D&D, Yu-Gi-Oh and Pokémon activities across the weekend. Check the live timetable because individual sessions can change.

## Tickets and entry

Online weekend tickets are £30 (down from £37 at the door), Saturday-only £22 and Sunday-only £15. Early Bird adds £6 for entry from 10:00, VIP adds £15 with Early Bird benefits; carers get 50% off. Standard entry is from 11:30, with on-the-door sales from 12:00 (from 14:00 if online sells out). Daytime areas wind down around 18:00, with the main stage continuing into the evening.

All ages are welcome; under-16s must be accompanied by an adult 18+, and children eight and under enter free. Some evening content is 18+. The venue is step-free with lifts, accessible toilets and seating; assistance dogs are welcome, cloakroom is around £1 and closes at 18:00.
