A short English-language poem becomes the starting point for this haiku and calligraphy workshop. After an introduction to haiku background, methods and rules, participants write their own English haiku. Misako then translates it into Japanese calligraphy for each attendee to copy onto paper with brush and ink.

## From poem to take-home work

The session combines writing with calligraphic practice, ending with a finished piece to take home. Hammond House presents it as a two-hour workshop for exploring haiku and Japanese calligraphy together.
