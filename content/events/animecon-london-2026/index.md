AnimeCon London brings two days of anime and manga culture to Olympia London’s Grand Hall on 3–4 October. The current venue listing combines J-pop and K-pop performance, fashion, cosplay, creator-focused Artist Alley, shopping and international guest appearances.

## What is confirmed

The event’s published programme is still at category level: named 2026 guests, a dated stage timetable, panels, screenings, workshops and detailed cosplay activities are **not published / confirm with organiser**. No official source opened during research established whether this is a returning edition, so this page describes it simply as the 2026 event.
