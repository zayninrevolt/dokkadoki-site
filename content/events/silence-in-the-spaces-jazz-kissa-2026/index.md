Tokyo Jazz Joints creator Philip Arneill brings the quiet, vinyl-centred world of Japan’s jazz kissa to Bloomsbury. His lecture follows the listening spaces where records are played through high-end audio systems and preservation of jazz culture is treated with near-religious attention.

## A culture of close listening

Since 2015, Arneill’s Tokyo Jazz Joints project has documented jazz kissa across Japan. Using photographs and stories from that work, he will trace their origins, their renewed international attention and what may come next for the culture.

Arneill is a Belfast-born photographer, researcher and writer who lived and worked in Japan for 20 years. His *Tokyo Jazz Joints* monograph was published by Kehrer Verlag in July 2023 and is now in its fourth worldwide edition; a Japanese edition followed from Seigensha in August 2025. He received a Fellowship of the Royal Photographic Society in 2024.
