The Barbican and Serious recreate the atmosphere of Peter Cat, the Tokyo jazz club Haruki Murakami ran from 1974 to 1981, in a weekend programme of Japanese jazz, spoken-word elements, commissioned visuals and new stage design.

Three groups appear: Junko Onishi Trio, Sachi Hayasaka / TReS, and The Blend. The Blend's bassist and founder Yoshio Suzuki died on 23 September 2026. The performances will proceed with the four remaining members: Kosuke Mine, Keisuke Nakamura, Hakuei Kim and Tamaya Honda. The programme includes reinterpreted jazz classics and original compositions linked to Murakami’s literary world. Murakami will not appear in person, although the official pages say he gave the project his blessing.

## Three approaches to Japanese jazz

Junko Onishi is a pianist and Berklee graduate whose career includes performances with Betty Carter, Joe Henderson, Jackie McLean and the Mingus Big Band. Her first solo piano album, *American Classics*, was released in 2026.

Sachi Hayasaka's TReS is a Tokyo-based family trio blending jazz improvisation with tango, Brazilian music and other international influences. The Blend approaches the programme as a contemporary acoustic quartet after Suzuki's death, carrying forward music from the group's albums *Five Dance* and *Expectation*.

Spoken-word elements, a new stage design and commissioned visuals connect the performances to Murakami's literary world and the atmosphere of his former club.
