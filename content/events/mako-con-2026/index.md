Mako Con takes over the Glasgow Grosvenor Hotel in the city's West End from 16–18 October for a not-for-profit, volunteer-run weekend aimed squarely at adults: cosplay, anime and gaming with adult programming, three nights of parties and an all-inclusive ticket.

## What's on

The main-stage Idol Fest runs Sunday evening with performers including hi.mawaribbon and SLVR Octave. The programme also includes a "Whose Line Is It Anime?" stage event, artist spotlights, panels, workshops, photo ops, video and board gaming, and a cosplay contest. The organisers stress "cosplay is not consent": ask before photographing or recording attendees.

## Tickets

Weekend tickets are £70 in advance through Ticket Tailor until 15 October, and everything—Idol Fest, panels, performances, gaming and parties—is included in that price. Day tickets have also been available via the organiser's socials. The event is strictly 18+.

## Staying and getting there

The Grosvenor sits opposite the Botanic Gardens near Byres Road, a short walk from Hillhead Subway. The hotel offers accessible rooms, wheelchair-accessible parking and paths to the lift, assistive listening devices, and holds Green Tourism Silver accreditation. Glasgow Queen Street and Central are both about two miles away; the airport is ten miles.
