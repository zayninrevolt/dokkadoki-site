## Access and travel

Outernet Live is unseated and publishes step-free access throughout, lifts, accessible toilets, a lowered bar and a limited accessibility viewing area. Essential companion tickets and queue assistance are available by advance arrangement through the venue. Tottenham Court Road is the nearest station, with step-free access from Exit 4. The venue has no parking; it identifies Blue Badge bays at Q-Park Soho and Q-Park Chinatown. Read Outernet Live's [accessibility information](https://hereldn.com/accessibility/) before attending.
