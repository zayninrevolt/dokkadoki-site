Japanese electronic music pioneer **Soichi Terada** brings a brand-new live set, *Sumo x Apes*, to Manchester's New Century Locker on Saturday 7 November. The venue describes the performance as a bridge between his work in jungle, house and video-game soundtracks.

## A new live set

This is billed as a new Soichi Terada performance rather than a standard DJ set. New Century has not published an event-specific running order, support act or stage time, so check the ticket and venue pages close to the date.

Terada's catalogue spans club music and game composition, making the mix of live electronics, house rhythms and soundtrack ideas the central attraction of this Manchester date.

## Tickets and entry

New Century links to an official [See Tickets booking page](https://newcentury.seetickets.com/event/soichi-terada-presents-sumo-x-apes/new-century-locker-manchester/3702237?src=website). The current ticket price, mandatory fees and live availability need checking with the seller. The event-specific age limit is not published on the accessible venue listing.

The Locker is primarily a standing room. New Century is cashless, permits one bag no larger than A4 per person, and operates a no re-entry policy after a ticket has been scanned. Door and final set times can change, so use the time printed on the ticket.

## Access and travel

New Century Locker is fully step-free and has an accessible toilet and a reserved access area. The venue offers one free carer or personal-assistant ticket where eligibility is established, and asks visitors to submit access requirements at least seven days before the show where possible.

Manchester Victoria station is approximately a five-minute walk away, while Shudehill tram stop and bus interchange are around two minutes away. See New Century's [visitor information](https://newcenturymcr.com/info) and [accessibility guidance](https://newcenturymcr.com/accessibility) before attending.
