Calligraphy master Yoko Takenami leads an autumn-themed workshop in brush, ink, line, rhythm and composition. Participants will create a Japanese calligraphy piece on a *shikishi* board to take home.

## Three workshop sessions

Choose from **11:00 to 12:15**, **13:00 to 14:15** or **15:00 to 16:15** on Saturday 21 November. Each session explores autumn-related words and characters, different Japanese writing styles and the expressive qualities of brushwork.

## Materials and experience

All tools, equipment and materials are supplied, and no previous calligraphy experience is required. Takenami is the author of *The Simple Art of Japanese Calligraphy* and has taught at institutions including the British Library.

## Booking and age guidance

The Japan Society member and accompanying-guest early-bird rate is **£20** through Sunday 11 October. The regular rate is **£25**, with non-member booking opening on Monday 12 October. Spaces are limited.

The workshop is suitable for ages **14 and over**. Participants aged 14 to 15 must attend with a parent or legal guardian. Those aged 16 to 17 may attend independently if emergency contact details are supplied.

A full refund is available only when cancellation is received by noon on **9 November 2026**. After that deadline, the organiser will attempt to resell the place and cannot promise a refund.

Parking and event-specific step-free access, quiet-space and companion arrangements are not published. See the [official workshop page](https://www.japansociety.org.uk/event?event=1148&eventcat=1) for current session availability and booking.
