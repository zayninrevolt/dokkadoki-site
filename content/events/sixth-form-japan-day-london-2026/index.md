Sixth Form Japan Day gives UK students a full day to explore Japanese language, culture, university courses and Japan-related careers. No previous knowledge of Japanese is required.

## Programme

The published example timetable includes university language workshops, taster lectures, drop-in cultural activities, a musical performance, an alumni careers talk, a student panel and a university fair. The programme runs from **10:30 to 16:30**. The organiser says exact timings may change.

Sixth Form students receive priority. Year 10 and Year 11 students may apply, subject to availability. Students can attend with teachers or independently, but school and parent or guardian permission is required.

## Booking and travel support

Entry is free and the booking deadline is **5 November 2026**. Places are initially limited to 18 per school. Failure to attend without cancelling by the end of **4 November 2026** will result in a **£5 fee per student**, invoiced to the registered school.

Limited travel bursaries of up to **£60** may be available to students who apply through their school. Contact the Japan Society education team for eligibility and availability.

## Food, travel and access

The event is at Birkbeck's Malet Street building, London WC1E 7HX. Attendees should use the entrance facing Torrington Square. Food is not provided, so attendees should bring or buy lunch in advance. The closest Underground options include Russell Square, Euston Square and Tottenham Court Road. Birkbeck has no visitor parking.

Step-free access, quiet-space and companion arrangements are not specified on the event page. Check those requirements with Birkbeck and the Japan Society before travelling. See the [official event page](https://www.japansociety.org.uk/event?event=1199&eventcat=4) for the booking form and latest programme.
