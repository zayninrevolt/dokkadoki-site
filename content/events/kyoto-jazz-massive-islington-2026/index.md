Kyoto Jazz Massive returns to London for the EFG London Jazz Festival with a ten-piece live lineup and the Echoes of a New Dawn Orchestra, also known as Jéroboam. The 22 November show brings together Japanese jazz, funk, broken beat, soulful house, cosmic grooves and electronic music at Islington Assembly Hall.

Shuya Okino and Vanessa Freeman lead the performance, with Indy Eka, Agyei Osei, Carel Cleril, Jean-Baptiste Palies, Eli Frot, Kevin Le Bellec, Roman Reidid and Sebastian Betancur completing the published lineup. The festival announcement points to new material and a full live ensemble. Doors are listed at 19:00, with the performance at 19:30.
